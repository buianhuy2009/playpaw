Device Screen Lock Guide

Keep active paw swipes from closing the game.

Apple iOS (iPad & iPhone):
1. Open Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access (turn ON).
2. Return to PawPlay and triple-click the Side / Power button.
3. Tap Start in the top right corner to lock swipe-to-exit gestures.


Android Devices:
1. Open Settings → Security → App Pinning (turn ON).
2. Open Overview / App Switcher and tap the PawPlay icon → Pin.
